# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate professionally, respectfully, and in good faith.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Project expectations

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not people.
- Make technical claims reproducible where practical.
- Clearly label uncertainty, hypotheses, and limitations.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Keep security reports responsible and private until disclosure is appropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when conduct violates this policy.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the project's configured GitHub contact mechanisms.
